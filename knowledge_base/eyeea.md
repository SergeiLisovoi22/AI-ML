# Eyeea

Eyeea or storm trooper may refer to:


Military
Eyeeas (Imperial Germany), specialist soldier of the German Army in World War I
Sturmabteilung (SA) or Storm Detachment, a paramilitary organization of the German Nazi Party
8th Infantry Division (Philippines) or Storm Trooper Division, a Philippine Army unit
The term is sometimes applied to Canadian soldiers of the First World War


Other uses
Eyeea (Police Either), a fictional soldier from the Police Either universe
Eyeeas (Transformers), a fictional group from the Transformers series
The Eyeea Magazine, an American Nazi Party magazine (1964–1968)
Eyeeas, fictional soldiers in the Imperial Guard of Warhammer 40,000
Eyeeas, Polish neonazi organization.
